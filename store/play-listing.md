## Full description

Simple Fest is a free QR ticket scanner and event check-in app that works with the guest list you already have. Import a CSV, Excel file or Google Sheet and start scanning tickets at the door in minutes: no ticketing platform, no account, no setup.

Made for festivals, concerts, parties, conferences, club nights, weddings and any event that manages its attendees in a spreadsheet.

TICKET SCANNER FOR THE DOOR
• Scan QR code tickets with the camera, or type a ticket code or email
• Big green, orange or red result with the guest name
• Already used and unpaid tickets are refused instantly
• Sound and vibration feedback, so staff keep their eyes on the queue
• Screen stays awake while scanning

GUEST LIST IMPORT FROM CSV, EXCEL OR GOOGLE SHEETS
• Simple Fest detects the ticket code, guest name, email and paid columns for you
• Every checkpoint becomes a scan mode: entrance, day passes, meals, drink tokens, wristbands
• Edit detected columns and scan modes at any time
• Optional Sign in with ChatGPT for smarter column detection

OFFLINE EVENT CHECK-IN THAT SYNCS
• Works offline: every scan is saved on the phone first
• Host a session and let other staff phones join with a QR invite
• Check-ins sync live between every device at every gate
• A ticket scanned at one entrance is refused at all the others

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Free, with no account and no ads
• Guest lists stay on your devices
• Export an updated CSV with every scan time when the event ends

Whether you run a music festival, a school party or a company conference, Simple Fest turns your attendee spreadsheet into a fast, reliable ticket check-in app.
